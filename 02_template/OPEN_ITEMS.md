# Open items

Questions raised and deliberately not acted on, each with the condition that would close it, so a
later session can tell a deferred question from a forgotten one. A question that comes up
mid-session goes here, not into that session's work.

Items keep their number for life. A closed item stays, with a dated line saying what closed it.
